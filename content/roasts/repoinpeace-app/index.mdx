# Repo in Peace: A Marketplace With No Way to Contact the Marketplace

**The site:** repoinpeace.app
**Built with:** Lovable
**Score: 70/100 - Grade C**

## First Impressions

A marketplace where dead startups go to be bought, with the tone of a funeral home. The concept is the best part of this site and the copy commits to it. I came to roast the code and stayed to read the listings.

## What I Found

**1. You cannot contact the marketplace.** The "contact" link has no destination. A marketplace where people buy and sell whole projects needs one working way to reach a human, and this is the link a buyer clicks before they pay.

*Repro:* Click "contact".

**2. A second link is also dead.** `apa_template` goes nowhere. It reads like a placeholder that shipped.

*Repro:* Click it.

**3. One button is wider than the phone.** At 390px a button measures 403px, so the page scrolls 13px sideways. Small, but it is the kind of thing that makes a page feel unfinished.

*Repro:* DevTools, 390x844, drag sideways.

**4. The Lovable badge is still in the page.** The "Edit with" element is present in the live DOM. On a site selling finished projects, it is a small tell that this one is not.

*Repro:* Inspect the DOM, search for "Edit with".

<Solution>
1. Point "contact" at a `mailto:` link or a form.
2. Wire up or remove `apa_template`.
3. Let the wide button wrap or reduce its padding on small screens.
4. Turn off the edit badge in Lovable's publish settings.
</Solution>

## What Works

The concept, the voice, and the commitment to the bit. There is a canonical tag and a custom social image, which is more than most sites at this stage.

## Verdict

70/100. Four small fixes from a clean pass. The humor is doing the work the polish has not caught up to yet.

**Overall Score: 70/100 - Grade C**
